Questions The ones people ask on the first call.
What does a first call cost?

Nothing. The 20-minute call is free. You tell us where you are, we tell you what we would do first and whether we are the right people for it.

Are the prices fixed?

Yes. Before any work starts you receive the scope and the price in writing. Prices exclude VAT.

Who does the work?

The two founders, Chani Galgut and Jasmine Beukema. Both are certified ISO 13485 lead auditors with hands-on QA/RA experience in medtech.

Can a subsidy pay part of it?

Sometimes. Dutch startups can in some cases use a voucher, for example the LifeSciences@Work Value Voucher through MedTechPartners. Ask us on the call and we will check it with you.

Can you build our whole quality system?

Yes, as a scoped project after the gap assessment. The roadmap shows what it takes, and we quote the build as a fixed price.

Do you work on site or remotely?

Both. Internal audits usually include time on site, and most other work runs remotely with your team.
