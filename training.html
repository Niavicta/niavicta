Questions The ones people ask on the first call.
Is the certificate an accredited qualification?

No. You receive a certificate of completion for your training records. It shows competence for your internal audit programme, and it is not an accredited lead auditor qualification.

Online or in person?

Both. Most groups prefer in person at their own office, and we also teach online.

In which language?

English or Dutch.

Can you tailor it to our company?

Yes. We build the exercises around your own procedures, so your team practises on the real thing.
