Questions The ones people ask on the first call.
Do we need to be a regulated company?

No. This work is for any growing company. Regulated companies also get their quality requirements built in.

Which tools do you work with?

Mostly Microsoft 365, SharePoint and Power Automate, and we build on the tools you already have wherever we can.

Do we have to take every phase?

No. Each phase ends with a finished deliverable, and you decide at every step whether to continue.

Do we have to stop working while you do this?

No. We work alongside your team a couple of days a week, and the business keeps running.
