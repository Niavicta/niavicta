Questions The ones people ask on the first call.
Do you travel to suppliers?

Yes. We audit on site across the Netherlands and the EU, with travel charged at cost. Remote audits are possible where the scope allows it.

Can you audit suppliers outside the EU?

Yes, remotely, or on site by agreement. We scope that on the first call.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes. We sign your NDA, and the supplier's where they ask for one.

What happens after the audit?

You receive graded findings with evidence and a supplier risk rating. If you want, we follow up the supplier's corrective actions until they are closed.
